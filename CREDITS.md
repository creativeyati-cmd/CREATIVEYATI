# Credits

## Images

The demo images in `public/` are taken from various design case studies on
[Behance](https://www.behance.net) and are used here purely as placeholder
content to show the carousel with real-looking work. They were picked at
random — the projects, brands and visuals belong entirely to their original
creators, and all credit goes to them.

These images are **not** covered by this repository's MIT license. Do not
reuse them in your own projects — replace them with your own work (see the
`PROJECTS` list in `lib/carousel/config.js`).

If you are the creator of one of these images and would like it credited by
name or removed, open an issue and it will be done right away.
